# Bounded verified-source fallback

The first exact-barcode page can be incomplete or a bot challenge. Challenge pages are unusable evidence: the app skips them, does not send them to the model, and never solves or bypasses their checks.

Within the existing maximum of three automatic page fetches, an incomplete verified page can be replaced by a richer independently verified page. After finding a verified page, additional requests are limited to exact-barcode search hits advertising explicit quantity or ABV information. Search text only suggests where to fetch; it never supplies accepted facts. Existing local parsers validate facts from the fetched pages to compare coverage. Replacement requires either a newly verified product identity when the selected page has none, or strictly greater supported core-field coverage without contradictions. A verified identity upgrade remains possible when quantity or ABV is disputed; scalar conflicts cannot supply or invalidate the product name. Contradictory verified product identities stop selection and remain unresolved. Scalar conflicts remain field-rejection metadata through review; disputed fields are removed from the extraction and cannot be silently filled by a later label. Facts from different pages are not combined.

If later pages are unavailable, mismatched or no richer, the original verified page remains selected. Quota/authentication/rate limits retain their existing stop behavior. The model runs only once against the final selected page. Diagnostics record selection and challenge reasons without exposing private label text.

Regression fixtures represent the reported patterns, not copies of the user's missing raw report: an incomplete exact-code page followed by a richer Russo-style specification, and an ambiguous result followed by a challenge and an independently verified Aperol-style specification. They also cover misleading snippets, mismatch, unavailable fallback and conflicting sizes.

Preview 27 follow-up fixtures use the supplied absent-title Markdown `# Bot check... One moment...` wording and the reported ambiguous Buycott → EANData challenge → verified Frootbat structured Aperol identity chain. Scalar-conflict fixtures are synthetic and separate: the actual conflict's origin was not established. Normal prose mentioning a bot check or taking one moment is not classified by the new heading rule. Previously verified conflicts from unselected pages still survive persistence and label merging.

Empty extraction diagnostics report `NO_SUPPORTED_FIELDS` rather than `CANDIDATES_READY`. This is a statement about accepted evidence, not proof of a model or recognition failure.
