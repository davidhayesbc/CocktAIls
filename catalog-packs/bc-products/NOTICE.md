The licence assignment is independently corroborated by the Government of Canada federated catalogue and official BC licence terms described below. This is not a claim that the boolean was resolved by the publisher.

The files are a retained snapshot downloaded on 2026-11-14, not a fresh live retrieval. Dataset metadata was modified 2026-10-14T17:30:04.945513; the CSV resource reports last_modified 2026-10-14T17:30:04.928730. The data's named reporting period remains July 2026. The live licence page could not be fetched during implementation; the original catalogue assignment and source snapshot were byte-verified. The distributed metadata is a minimal projection retaining source IDs, resource URLs/dates and licence fields; incidental contact names, email addresses and administrative identifiers are omitted.

No government endorsement is implied. Product names and classifications remain source claims; availability, prices, current product specifications, alcohol-free status, and allergen safety are not guaranteed. No retail website text, images, logos, or private household data are included.

Transformations: numeric UPC strings are preserved alongside check-digit-validated zero-padded GTIN-14 candidates. Ambiguous short codes, duplicate keys, non-beverage classifications, invalid codes and non-single-container packages are retained but excluded from automatic matching. Decimal litres are displayed as millilitres for eligible single containers. Brand is never inferred from product names. The raw CSV retains the original values, including historical prices; prices are not used by the app.
