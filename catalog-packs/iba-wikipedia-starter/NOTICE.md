Its license identifies the CSV and archived Wikipedia HTML as CC BY-SA 3.0; its code is separately MIT licensed.

The pack contains only the factual ingredient and quantity fields from these pinned article revisions. CocktAIls authored the concise preparation methods and normalized structured ingredient labels. The original source ingredient strings and any quantity normalization are recorded per recipe in `recipes.json`. No images, explanatory/history prose, `iba-web/` content, or other cocktail records are included. In particular, the Whiskey Sour source row does not list egg white or garnish, so neither is added here.
